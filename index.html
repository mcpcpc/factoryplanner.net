FAQ

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no trials and no paid tiers. Hosting is covered by donations from people who find it useful.

Do I need CAD experience?

No. If you can sketch a floor plan on paper, you can draw one here. The drafting details are handled for you when you export.

Where is my work saved?

Sign in with Google and your layouts are saved to your account, so you can pick up where you left off on any computer.

What can I export?

Print-ready drawings as SVG or PNG, and DXF files that open in AutoCAD and most other CAD tools. DXF layers follow the US National CAD Standard.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes. Anyone can post ideas, feature requests and bug reports on GitHub.
