How it works

From an empty floor to a layout that works.

  1. Draw your floor

    Walls, doors, columns and machines, on every level of the building, right in your browser.

  2. Map how materials move

    Connect machines and say how much travels between them. Busy routes stand out at a glance.

  3. Get a better layout

    One click suggests an arrangement that cuts wasted travel. Keep it, tweak it, or undo it.

  4. Share the plan

    Export clean drawings and CAD files your team and contractors can build from.

FAQ

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no trials and no paid tiers. Hosting is covered by donations from people who find it useful.

Do I need an account?

Not to try it. The demo works on any device, with no sign-up, and lets you download your project file. Sign in with an account you already have to save layouts and run the optimiser.

Do I need CAD experience?

No. If you can sketch a floor plan on paper, you can draw one here. The drafting details are handled for you when you export.

What can I export?

Print-ready drawings as SVG or PNG, and DXF files that open in AutoCAD and most other CAD tools. DXF layers follow the US National CAD Standard.
